# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Everyone who looks Matias Nahuel Araujo up, all of them confirmed as audiences:

- **Employers and recruiters** evaluating him for innovation, R&D, product or strategy roles.
- **Clients** (companies or founders) considering him for a specific consulting project.
- **Potential partners** for ventures of his own, in the spirit of the Mingo MBT project.
- **Community and peers** in tech and innovation who read what he writes (Notes).

Typical situation: they arrive from LinkedIn, a shared link or a search, skim on a phone or laptop, and decide within a minute whether he is worth contacting.

## Product Purpose

A personal site at `matiasaraujo.ar` that shows who he is and makes it easy to start a conversation. Success: the visitor understands that he combines hands-on engineering (electronics, R&D, prototypes) with business judgment (MBT), finds proof of both, and reaches out.

## Positioning

An Electronics Engineer who has spent his career in innovation and R&D (fintech, SIM cards, blockchain, AI) and now holds a Master in Business & Technology. He can both build the proof of concept and make the business case for it, and he asks "what do you need it for?" before building anything.

## Operating Context

- Contact happens through LinkedIn, email (matias.araujo@outlook.com) and GitHub.
- Content is bilingual: English markup by default, Spanish via the language switch.
- Notes are written by him as Markdown files in the repository, created from GitHub's web editor or by asking Claude.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site on GitHub Pages; no paid services and no backend.
- Single `index.html` with embedded CSS/JS; Notes use the Jekyll build GitHub Pages already runs.
- Business capabilities to highlight (confirmed): strategy and business models; finance (business cases, projections, valuation, investment analysis).
- Undecided: whether to add market/go-to-market and product/data capabilities later.

## Brand Commitments

- Motto, exact wording: "The world we live in is what we make it." Shown without attribution (he first saw it at a talk at Permissionless 2022, Palm Beach).
- Voice from his own LinkedIn summary: curious, informal, a bit self-deprecating ("grew up taking things apart… big air quotes on putting them back together"), allergic to hype.
- Hidden easter egg for his high-school awards stays hidden; do not surface them as achievements.

## Evidence on Hand

- Work history and education: LinkedIn profile export (reflected in the Experience and Education sections).
- MBT final project: Mingo (mingo.ar), an agricultural crowdfunding platform; business model and landing page built, never launched.
- Paper: "Evaluación y detección de potenciales evocados sobre una interfaz cerebro computadora" (ARANDUCON 2016), PDF at `assets/papers/`.
- Profile photo at `assets/images/profile-photo.jpg`.
- Absent, do not fabricate: client testimonials, logos of clients, metrics or results of consulting work, published notes.

## Product Principles

1. Real over hype: every claim traces back to something he did.
2. Builder and business: always show both sides together.
3. Personality is the differentiator; generic portfolio patterns undersell him.
4. Easy to contact from anywhere on the page.
