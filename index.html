About me

I grew up taking things apart, figuring out how they worked, and putting them back together (big air quotes on that last part). That curiosity never left, it just scaled: from watches to systems, from circuits to AI.

Today I work in innovation as an R&D Specialist at Flock IT, but not the "let's implement this tech because it's trendy" kind. I'd rather ask "what do you need it for?" before building. I believe the best technology is the one that solves real problems, not the one that follows the hype.

I enjoy fixing things (most recently, an electric car charger), learning from people from other cultures, and finding the "how does it work" in everything, from complex systems to financial markets. To me, "it can't be done" means "well, it couldn't be done before, now let's see how".
